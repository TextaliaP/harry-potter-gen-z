The path opened onto a great black lake. Across the way, a vast castle with sparkly windows sat on a mountain.
"Four to a boat!" Hagrid called, pointing to a bunch of boats in the water. Harry, Ron, Neville, and Hermione piled into one.
"Everyone in?" Hagrid yelled. "Let's GO!" 🛶
The boats moved across the glassy lake. Everyone stared up at the castle. It towered over them as they got closer.
"Heads DOWN!" Hagrid yelled. They ducked, and the boats went through a curtain of ivy into a wide opening in the cliff. They went along a dark tunnel that took them under the castle until they reached an underground harbor, where they climbed onto the rocks.
"Yo, is this your toad?" Hagrid asked, checking the boats.🐸
